RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made on the 1st of August, 2024, by and between:
Landlord: Mr. Olamide Johnson, residing at 14 Victoria Island, Lagos
Tenant: Miss Sarah Adeyemi

1. Property Description
The Landlord agrees to lease to the Tenant the 2-bedroom apartment located at Flat 4, 12B Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos ("Premises").

2. Lease Term
The lease will begin on August 15, 2024, and continue for a period of one (1) year.

3. Rent
The total rent for the year is ₦3,500,000 (Three Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira). The rent is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances whatsoever, even if the Premises become uninhabitable due to natural disasters, fire, or structural collapse not caused by the Tenant.

4. Service Charge & Maintenance
The Tenant shall pay an annual service charge of ₦800,000. This covers security, waste disposal, and general maintenance of common areas. The Landlord reserves the absolute right to increase the service charge at any time during the tenancy without prior notice to the Tenant, and the Tenant must pay the increased amount within 7 days of demand.

5. Use of Premises
The Premises shall be used exclusively as a private residence. Any commercial activity is strictly prohibited. The Tenant may not sublet the Premises or assign this Agreement.

6. Inspection and Entry
The Landlord or their agents may enter the Premises at any time of day or night to inspect the property or perform repairs, without requiring any prior notice or consent from the Tenant.

7. Termination
If the Tenant breaches any provision of this Agreement, the Landlord may immediately terminate this Agreement and forcefully evict the Tenant without applying to any court or serving any statutory eviction notices.

8. Repairs
The Tenant is responsible for all repairs inside the apartment, including plumbing, electrical faults, and structural issues that arise during the tenancy, regardless of whether they were pre-existing conditions.
